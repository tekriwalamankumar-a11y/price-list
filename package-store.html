Package 02 · Website with checkout

Online Store

A complete online shop: customers can browse, add products to a cart, check out, and submit orders. No separate CRM or offline shop software is required for this package.

Website and product presentation

  • Branded website pages with layouts for phone, tablet and desktop screens.
  • Home page, shop navigation and agreed information pages.
  • Product categories and product pages for the agreed launch range.
  • Product photos, names, descriptions, prices and other supplied details.
  • Clear product prices and product information before checkout.
  • Contact, delivery and shop information sections using details supplied by the business.

Customer shopping and checkout

  • Customers can browse the catalogue and open product details.
  • Add products to the cart, review selections and remove items before ordering.
  • Adjust product quantities in the cart.
  • See an order summary before submitting checkout.
  • Enter the customer and delivery details required to fulfil the order.
  • Complete checkout using the agreed payment option/provider.
  • Receive an on-screen confirmation after submitting an order.

Shop order handling

  • New website orders appear in the shop’s order area for review.
  • Staff can accept an order and update its progress.
  • Order stages include received, accepted, dispatched and completed.
  • The shop can record a customer’s return request for follow-up.
  • Order details are available to help staff confirm the items, customer and delivery information.
  • The shop handles customer questions, fulfilment and return decisions directly.

Package limits and separate charges

  • This package is a complete website store and does not require the separate offline software licence.
  • It does not include the offline inventory, supplier, staff-management or CRM suite in Package 03.
  • Payment provider transaction charges, domain and hosting renewals, and paid third-party services are separate.
  • GST is charged separately.
Order tracking scope

Order tracking in this package means the shop can update the agreed order stages in its order area. A courier-company live tracking connection or automatic SMS/email alerts are not included unless agreed separately.
