Package 03 · Website + offline shop software

Online Store + Shop Software

Everything in Package 02, plus a separate offline operations application for the owner and shop staff. Website orders sync to the local shop system when internet is available.

Online store features

  • All website, catalogue, cart, checkout, payment option and order-handling features in Package 02.
  • Customers can place online orders without contacting the shop through WhatsApp to complete checkout.
  • Shop staff can receive orders, update progress, complete orders and record return requests.

Owner and employee access

  • A master owner account to manage access to the shop software.
  • Separate employee accounts assigned by the owner.
  • Staff sign in with their own assigned credentials.
  • Access can be arranged by role for owner and employee tasks.
  • Shop activity is managed within the business’s local system.

Customer and order records

  • Customer contact details associated with shop and website orders.
  • Order records available to help staff review and process work.
  • Customer follow-up notes for service and order handling.
  • Order status updates from the website can be reflected in the shop workflow.

Inventory and suppliers

  • Maintain product and stock records for the shop.
  • Review stock levels while processing customer orders.
  • Keep supplier details and supplier order records.
  • Record incoming stock and related shop operations.

Offline use and website connection

  • The shop can use its local records and shop workflows on its local computer/network without continuous internet access.
  • Internet is needed to connect the local software to the online store and receive new online order updates.
  • When connected, agreed order and stock information can sync between the website and the shop software.
  • If internet is unavailable, the website remains online but new website orders will not reach the local software until the connection returns.
  • The business keeps and manages its local software records and its own backups.

Separate costs and limits

  • The local computer and network equipment are supplied by the business.
  • Domain, website hosting, payment-provider charges and paid third-party services are separate.
  • GST is charged separately.
Software licence and renewal

The offline software licence is ₹10,000 for the first year. From year two onward, the renewal fee is ₹3,000 per year + GST. The website build is a one-time fee; domain and hosting renewals are separate.
