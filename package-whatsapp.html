Package 01 · Website only

WhatsApp Website

A branded website for customers to explore products and send an order enquiry directly to WhatsApp Business.

Website presentation

  • A website styled around the business name, logo, chosen colours and supplied brand material.
  • A home page that introduces the shop and guides customers to its products.
  • Collection or category sections to make products easier to browse.
  • Product displays using the photos, names, descriptions and prices supplied for the agreed launch range.
  • About, contact and other agreed information sections.
  • Clear navigation between the main website sections.
  • Contact details, business hours, address and supplied social links where requested.
  • Layout adapted for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
  • Basic page titles and descriptions that help search and link previews identify the site.
  • Footer with useful shop information and navigation links.

WhatsApp order journey

  • Order buttons that open a conversation with the shop’s WhatsApp Business number.
  • Product-specific buttons can include the product name in the prefilled enquiry message.
  • Customers can ask questions, confirm options and discuss delivery with the shop in the chat.
  • The shopkeeper confirms availability, final amount, payment and delivery directly with the customer.
  • The customer and shopkeeper continue the order conversation in WhatsApp Business.

Package limits

  • No website cart, checkout or payment collection on the site.
  • No website order dashboard, automatic order status or customer CRM.
  • Orders are not automatically stored in a website order system; the shop manages enquiries in WhatsApp.
Separate costs

One-time domain and hosting setup or renewal costs, WhatsApp charges if any, and third-party services are separate. GST is charged separately.
